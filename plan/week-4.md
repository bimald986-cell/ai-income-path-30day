# Week 4 – Launch & Get First Sales

**Goal:** Get the first paid customers.

---

### Launch Day (choose one day early in the week)

**Minimum launch actions (free):**
- [ ] Post in 3–5 relevant Reddit communities (follow their rules carefully)
- [ ] Post on X/Twitter
- [ ] Post in any Discord servers you are in (Claude, Cursor, freelance, indie hacker)
- [ ] Share with your personal network / WhatsApp / Telegram if relevant
- [ ] Post on Indie Hackers (optional but good)

**Posting style that works better:**
- Share the problem first
- Show a real example of one skill working
- Be honest that it is v1
- Link to the sales page

---

### Days after Launch

**Daily:**
- [ ] Reply to every comment and question
- [ ] Track what people ask / complain about
- [ ] Make small improvements if needed
- [ ] Post in 1–2 new places if energy allows

**Do not** spend money on ads yet.

---

### If You Get Zero Sales in First 7 Days

We will diagnose together:
1. Is the offer clear?
2. Is the price right?
3. Are we talking to the right people?
4. Does the product actually feel valuable when someone tries it?

Then we adjust quickly.

---

### End of 30 Days

Even if revenue is small, you will have:
- A real product
- Real feedback
- A process you can repeat for the next pack
- Proof you can ship

That is the foundation we will use to expand.
