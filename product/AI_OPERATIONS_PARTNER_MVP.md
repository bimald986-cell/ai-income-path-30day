# AI Operations Partner MVP — Self-Validation Specification

**Status:** Day 4 product hypothesis locked for MVP build  
**Mission:** Build one integrated AI Operations Partner, then use its own market-research and outreach capability to discover, test, and win its first real paying customer.

## 1. Product hypothesis

The product is not a bundle of unrelated writing prompts. It is an evidence-driven operating skill/system for a solo operator or small team.

Its core loop is:

**Observe → Gather → Understand → Filter → Prioritize → Explain → Act within authority → Verify → Measure → Report → Learn**

The product should earn authority through evidence, not confidence.

## 2. Core capability A — Evidence-Based Work Prioritizer

The system gathers relevant information from authorized sources such as email, tasks, notes, documents, repositories, operating standards, agents, market observations, and prior decisions.

It must:
- understand the available information before ranking work;
- separate immediate attention from important/non-urgent work, routine noise, and operational-improvement opportunities;
- evaluate the likely outcome/value of each action;
- evaluate consequences of delay and dependencies;
- evaluate financial, operational, reputational, compliance, relationship, and execution risk;
- rank recommended work;
- explain why each important ranking was made;
- cite or identify the evidence used;
- state missing/conflicting/stale evidence and confidence;
- compare why a higher-ranked item outranks alternatives;
- recommend an action while leaving consequential owner decisions visible.

The system may disagree with the owner, but it must explain the disagreement with evidence rather than merely asserting authority.

## 3. Core capability B — Recurring Work Operator

The system should identify recurring work and complete as much as safely possible without repeatedly asking for obvious approvals.

Initial desired use cases include:
- read, classify, summarize, and prioritize authorized email;
- prepare replies and extract actions from email;
- run approved recurring content-production workflows such as Mind & Mythos production;
- inspect authorized GitHub repositories and work on approved backlog/maintenance items;
- conduct market research;
- identify and qualify legitimate prospective customers;
- prepare personalized outreach;
- maintain an outreach/follow-up pipeline;
- prepare the daily operating plan.

### Authority levels

**Observe:** read, analyze, classify, research, compare, and report.

**Act within guardrails:** perform reversible, explicitly delegated recurring work and prepare internal artifacts/drafts.

**Approval required:** first-time or consequential external communications, spending, contractual/financial commitments, destructive actions, sensitive decisions, major production deployments, major architecture changes, or actions outside explicit delegation.

Autonomy can expand only after quality is measured and the owner deliberately delegates that class of action.

## 4. Core capability C — Market Intelligence & Outreach

The MVP's first mission is to help find its own market and first customer.

Initial segment hypotheses, in owner's current judgment order:
1. Solo consultant or coach
2. Creator running content, sponsorships, and products
3. Tiny agency owner with 2–5 employees
4. Small online-business/SaaS founder

These are hypotheses, not conclusions.

The research engine should actively try to disprove the leading hypothesis rather than collect only confirming evidence.

For each segment it should evaluate:
- frequency of the operating problem;
- severity/cost of the problem;
- evidence of information overload and prioritization difficulty;
- recurring administrative burden;
- market/outreach burden;
- existing alternatives and competitors;
- existing spending or willingness-to-pay signals;
- AI fit and integration feasibility;
- trust/autonomy objections;
- reachable acquisition channels.

### Prospect workflow

**Discover → Qualify → Find evidence of fit → Score → Explain score → Draft personalized outreach → Owner approval → Send through an authorized channel → Track response → Learn**

No fabricated identities, fake testimonials, deceptive personalization, private/illicit data, or spam-style bulk outreach.

## 5. Self-selling validation experiment

The product becomes its own first case study.

### Question

Can the AI Operations Partner use its own research, prioritization, and outreach capabilities to identify a real target market and contribute to acquiring its first paying customer?

### Experiment stages

1. Research the four initial customer hypotheses.
2. Produce a comparison based on evidence, including reasons against each segment.
3. Select a small initial segment and explain the choice.
4. Define a narrow offer and testable value proposition.
5. Identify a small batch of legitimate, well-matched prospects from lawful/public or owner-authorized sources.
6. Prepare individualized outreach based on actual evidence of fit.
7. Owner reviews the first outbound batch.
8. Track delivery, responses, positive responses, objections, meetings/conversations, trials, and paid conversions.
9. Change targeting/message/offer based on evidence.
10. Preserve the complete experiment as a case study whether it succeeds or fails.

## 6. Morning operating briefing

The operator should make the business understandable in about two minutes.

It should report:
- **Product today:** current performance and meaningful movement.
- **Market today:** relevant market conditions and competitor/customer changes.
- **Observations:** evidence-backed developments that could affect the product or market.
- **Last 24 hours:** meaningful work completed and verified.
- **Outreach funnel:** qualified prospects, approved contacts, contacts made, responses, positive responses, follow-ups, conversions.
- **Outcomes:** value created rather than task count alone.
- **Risks/blocks:** what could hurt progress or requires intervention.
- **Needs owner:** only decisions/approvals genuinely requiring the owner.
- **Today's plan:** prioritized actions with reasons.

## 7. Outcome metrics

Do not optimize for activity volume.

Primary validation metrics:
- qualified prospects found;
- response rate;
- positive-response rate;
- conversations/demos/trials generated;
- paid conversions and revenue;
- hours of owner work saved;
- recurring work completed correctly;
- errors/rework;
- important risks detected/prevented;
- owner overrides of prioritization and why;
- evidence that changes the target-market hypothesis.

**30-day minimum commercial validation remains: at least one real paid sale.**

A zero-sale result is still informative if the experiment records where the funnel failed and what evidence changed.

## 8. Trust and evidence standard

For consequential recommendations, preserve:
- recommendation;
- evidence used;
- source/authority of evidence;
- expected outcome;
- risk of acting;
- risk of delaying/not acting;
- uncertainty/missing information;
- confidence where useful;
- alternatives considered;
- approval status;
- action taken;
- result.

Never manufacture confidence. Never claim an external action happened unless it can be verified.

## 9. MVP boundary

This MVP is a skill/system specification, not a claim that one prompt can directly control email, GitHub, publishing, or outreach platforms.

Actual actions require available integrations, credentials, permissions, and platform-compliant workflows. When a required integration is absent, the operator should prepare the work and clearly state what cannot yet be executed.

## 10. Day 5 build target

Build the first executable version around the **Evidence-Based Work Prioritizer + Market Validation Engine** because these capabilities can test the product hypothesis before every external integration exists.

Initial executable input:
- a collection of tasks/signals/market evidence/prospect evidence.

Initial executable output:
1. classified signal/noise/improvement items;
2. evidence-backed priority ranking;
3. risks and consequences of delay;
4. market-segment hypothesis scoring;
5. recommended next actions;
6. approval requirements;
7. concise operating briefing.

Then test it against realistic scenarios before enabling external outreach.

## 11. Definition of Day 4 completion

Day 4 is complete when:
- the integrated MVP direction is documented;
- its authority/approval model is explicit;
- the self-selling experiment is defined;
- initial market hypotheses are recorded as hypotheses;
- outcome metrics are defined;
- Day 5 has a concrete first executable build target.

This supersedes the earlier requirement to lock 6–8 unrelated skills. The change is evidence-driven and should itself remain part of the learning record.
